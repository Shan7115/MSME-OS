# MSMEOS2 — Business Model & Lean Canvas

## 1. Problem
MSMEs generate substantial data across project profiles, loan dossiers, and tax filings, but lack the bandwidth and analytical tools to detect latent working-capital traps, excessive customer concentration, or non-compliant documentation prior to bank audits.

## 2. Customer Segments
- **Beachhead Segment:** Indian Manufacturing & Agro-processing MSMEs (Turnover ₹1 Crore – ₹25 Crores) seeking bank term loan expansions or working capital restructuring.
- **Secondary Segment:** Practicing Chartered Accountants & Financial Advisors managing loan syndication portfolios.
- **Institutional Segment:** MSME Incubators, NBFC Credit Underwriters, and District Industries Centres (DICs).

## 3. Value Proposition
- Upload business documents already on hand—zero manual spreadsheet entry required.
- Strict evidence attribution: every risk connects to an explicit page and line citation.
- Prioritized 30/60/90-day action plan that unlocks liquidity and addresses lender covenants.

## 4. Channels & Distribution
- Strategic partnerships with Regional MSME Trade Associations (e.g. CODISSIA, TANSTIA).
- Chartered Accountant referral network with rev-share affiliate incentives.
- Direct digital inbound marketing focused on Udyam credit guarantee readiness.

## 5. Revenue Streams
- **Starter (Free):** Single document basic diagnostic report (conversion top-of-funnel).
- **MSME Growth (₹2,499 / month):** Deep multi-format document analysis, full 30/60/90 action plans, and printable board dossiers.
- **Advisor Pro (₹6,999 / month):** Multi-client workspaces (up to 25 enterprises) with white-labeled reports.
- **Enterprise (Custom):** On-premise / local-first deployment with custom ERP and NBFC underwriting connectors.

## 6. Cost Structure
- Cloud Compute & Encrypted Storage: ~20%
- Document Processing & NLP Inference: ~25%
- Cluster Sales & CA Partner Acquisition: ~35%
- Security Compliance & ISO Audits: ~20%
