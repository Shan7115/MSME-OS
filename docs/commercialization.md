# MSMEOS2 — Commercialization & Go-To-Market Plan

## 1. Beachhead Market Focus
The initial commercial beachhead concentrates on the Western Tamil Nadu industrial corridor (Coimbatore, Tirupur, Erode, Salem). This geography possesses over 80,000 registered MSMEs in food processing, textile engineering, and light fabrication with heavy reliance on bank Cash Credit facilities.

## 2. Customer Acquisition Funnel
1. **Free Health Check On-Ramp:** MSME owners upload an existing Detailed Project Report or provisional balance sheet to obtain an immediate 0–100 Readiness Score and top 3 findings.
2. **Action Plan Paywall / Retainer Conversion:** To unlock the full 30/60/90-day liquidity action plan, factoring recommendations, and board-ready reports, users subscribe to the ₹2,499/mo Growth Plan.
3. **CA Channel Multiplier:** Independent accounting firms receive white-labeled reporting portals under the Advisor Pro tier (₹6,999/mo), enabling them to manage up to 25 client dossiers.

## 3. Pilot Deployment Strategy
- Secure cohort of 25 manufacturing MSMEs across the Erode turmeric and agro-processing cluster.
- Measure primary ROI metric: reduction in debtor days (DSO) and successful commercial bank term loan sanction turnaround time.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Target < ₹4,500 via CA partnership referrals.
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Target > ₹28,000 (average 12-month retention).
- **Payback Period:** < 2 months.
