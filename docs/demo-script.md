# MSMEOS2 — 3-to-5 Minute Demonstration Walkthrough Script

This script outlines the exact demo flow designed for evaluators and reviewers per Section 12 and 51 of `PRD.md`.

---

## Step 1: Open the Application (0:00 - 0:30)
1. Ensure the server is running (`python app.py`).
2. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8000` in the web browser.
3. **Narration:**
   > *"Welcome to MSMEOS2. MSMEs generate mountains of paperwork—bank loan project reports, operational schedules, tax statements—yet crucial signals often stay buried. MSMEOS2 is built on a simple premise: Business Document → Structured Understanding → Evidence → Decisions → Prioritized Action."*

---

## Step 2: Show Initial Dashboard State (0:30 - 1:00)
1. Point to the clean empty state dashboard: 0 documents, no fabricated metrics.
2. Click **"Try Demo Flow"** on the sidebar or top banner.
3. Observe the progressive 4-stage processing animation:
   - Reading & Parsing Document
   - Extracting Schedules & Facts
   - Evaluating Risk Signals
   - Synthesizing 30/60/90 Action Plan.

---

## Step 3: Executive Decision Dashboard (1:00 - 2:00)
1. The dashboard immediately updates for **Sri Murugan Agro Foods & Spices Pvt. Ltd.** (Erode, Tamil Nadu).
2. Point to the **59/100 Readiness Score**:
   - Show transparent scoring breakdown: Strong financial visibility (72%), but acute vulnerabilities in Customer Risk (42%) and Working Capital (46%).
3. Point to the **Enterprise Snapshot**:
   - Annual turnover ₹142.50 Lakhs, FSSAI certified, CC Limit ₹20.00 Lakhs, DSO: 78.4 days.

---

## Step 4: Findings Explorer & Evidence Attribution (2:00 - 3:00)
1. Navigate to **Findings Explorer**.
2. Filter by **Critical** severity and click on the first finding:
   - *High Customer Concentration Exposure (38.0% Share)*.
3. Open the detail view and emphasize:
   - **Document Citation:** Exact text from Page 3 showing Kaveri Hypermarkets ₹54.15 Lakhs and 88-day payment realization.
   - **Why It Matters:** Liquidity exposure and working capital lockup.
   - **Remediation:** TReDS invoice discounting.
4. Click **"In Progress"** to demonstrate workflow state persistence.

---

## Step 5: Executive Report & 30/60/90-Day Plan (3:00 - 4:00)
1. Navigate to **Executive Report**.
2. Show the board-ready diagnostic dossier, evidence citations, and 30/60/90-day prioritized framework.
3. Click **"Print / Save as PDF"** to demonstrate the clean print stylesheet.

---

## Step 6: Validation Hub, Business Model & Roadmap (4:00 - 5:00)
1. Open **Validation Hub**: Show zero fabrication policy, live stakeholder response collection form, and metrics.
2. Open **Business Model**: Show Lean Canvas, beachhead targeting, and commercial pricing tiers (Free, Growth, Advisor, Enterprise).
3. Open **Roadmap**: Review the 4-phase trajectory from local MVP to institutional credit syndication.
4. Click **"Reset"** in the sidebar to prove repeatable demonstration runs.
